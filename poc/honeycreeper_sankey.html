Hawaiian Honeycreeper Extinction — Ribbon Phylogeny

A chronological, ribbon-style Sankey of ~60 honeycreeper lineages radiating from the Asian rosefinch (Carpodacus) and thinning to extinction.

Each ribbon is one lineage: it starts where it split off (divergence time, in mya) and tapers to a point where it went extinct; ribbons still alive run to the right edge. Width = population tier (log-scaled); color = the project's era scheme. The x-axis is broken — left panel = deep time (linear mya), right panel = the last ~500 years (linear CE) — so the entire extinction crisis is legible, not a hairline. Hover a ribbon for its record.

Methodology

Why "ribbon Sankey," not d3-sankey. A standard Sankey conserves flow (width(parent)=Σwidth(children)), which is false in evolution — a small founding population can radiate into a large clade. So each edge is a tapering filled ribbon: width ∝ the tier at divergence, thinning to zero at the extinction year. The shape of the taper carries the signal; absolute width is a tier, not a census.

Population tiers (log-scaled widths). abundant 24px · common 16px · rare 10px · critical 5px · extinct → 0. Pre-1970 widths are a qualitative size/range prior (AVOTREX body mass + island range), flagged as such in each tooltip — interpretive, not quantitative.

Backbone. Topology from Campana et al. (2026, PNAS) mitogenome tree (Figshare doi:10.25573/data.28914329, HOFI_MT_x70_d3.tree); node ages anchored to Lerner et al. (2011, Current Biology 21:1838) + Wikipedia (2026): ancestor–Carpodacus 7.2 mya, poʻouli 5.7 mya, creeper clade 4.7 mya, primary-clade "big bang" 4.0 mya.

Data difficulties (flagged, not hidden)

  • Extinction years conflict across sources. Maui ʻākepa (1983 vs 1988), ʻōʻū (1989 vs 1998), ʻula-ʻai-hāwane (1892 vs 1937), Koa-finches (1891/1896 Wikipedia vs pre-1795 James & Prince 2008). Each is called out in its tooltip ⚠ data difficulty.
  • AVONET still lists 5 extinct species as "Extant." Maui nukupuu, Kauaʻi nukupuu, Maui ʻākepa, Oʻahu ʻalauahio, ʻōʻū — a taxonomic snapshot, not conservation status. Re-keyed here as extinct.
  • Subfossil-only lineages have no DNA. Rendered with a dashed outline so molecular-clock inference reads apart from subfossil evidence.
  • Two genus keys changed in Campana 2026. ʻiʻiwi = Vestiaria_coccinea (AVONET Drepanis_coccinea); ʻalawī = Loxops_mana (AVONET Manucerthia_mana). Both keys recorded in the CSV.
