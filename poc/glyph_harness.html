Honeycreeper glyph sampler

Every silhouette is generated by assets/hc_glyphs.js from the species' measurements (wing → body length, mass → body depth and head size, tail, tarsus, bill length and depth). Bill curvature and the short lower mandible of the nukupuʻu and ʻākiapōlāʻau come from qualitative columns in data/honeycreeper_nodes.csv. In the chart, extinct birds lie on their backs (gray, belly up, so a decurved bill curves up) and survivors fly (dark).
